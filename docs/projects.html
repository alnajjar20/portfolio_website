Multilingual Campus Resource Guide

Role: Team Lead & Translator (Arabic/English)
Course: BUS 3000 – Professional Communication
Timeline: September 2025 – December 2025

Challenge: Many international students on campus struggled to find basic services (health center, tutoring, housing assistance) due to language barriers and fragmented information.

My Role:
- Led a team of 4 students to create a trilingual resource guide (English, Arabic, Spanish)
- Translated all content into Arabic and coordinated with Spanish-speaking teammate
- Presented the guide to the university’s International Student Office for feedback

Result:
- Guide distributed to 150+ students during orientation week
- Received positive feedback from international student advisors
- Project earned an A grade and was recommended for wider university use

Skills Demonstrated:
Multilingual translation • Team leadership • Cross-cultural communication • Project coordination

Team Collaboration Case Study: Group Problem-Solving Simulation

Role: Facilitator & Note-taker
Course: MHR 3010 – Organizational Behavior
Timeline: February 2026 – April 2026

Challenge: A 5-member team had to solve a complex business simulation involving resource allocation, deadline pressure, and conflicting viewpoints among members.

My Role:
- Acted as primary facilitator, ensuring all voices were heard
- Documented decisions and action items in shared Google Docs
- Mediated a disagreement between two members by proposing a compromise based on data from the simulation

Result:
- Team completed simulation two days ahead of deadline
- Earned 95% on the final report
- Peer feedback praised my ability to “keep everyone focused and respected”

Skills Demonstrated:
Conflict mediation • Meeting facilitation • Documentation • Adaptability under pressure

Rancho Cucamonga High School Alumni Panel: Pathways to College Success

Role: Panelist & Co-Organizer
Type: Community Service / Leadership
Timeline: March 2026

Challenge: Current high school students needed relatable, multilingual advice about transitioning to a four-year university, especially from first-generation backgrounds.

My Role:
- Co-organized a panel of 6 alumni now at Cal Poly Pomona, UC Riverside, and Chaffey College
- Shared personal journey from high school to university, emphasizing study habits and resource use
- Answered questions in English and Spanish for bilingual attendees

Result:
- 40+ high school students attended
- Post-event survey showed 92% felt more confident about applying to college
- Invited back for fall 2026 session

Skills Demonstrated:
Public speaking (bilingual) • Event coordination • Mentorship • Community engagement
